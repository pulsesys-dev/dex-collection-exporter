Not affiliated with Dex. This page runs in your browser and only reads the collection data Dex already shows on public profiles. Excel and PDF exports load a small library from cdnjs the first time you use them.

Prices are what Dex reports from each market. TCGplayer and eBay are usually USD and Cardmarket is EUR. Avg Price is the average of the markets that have one, so totals won't match the Dex app exactly.

Troubleshooting

"Profile not found": check the username, and that the profile is set to public in Dex.
"Request failed": Dex may have updated its app. Try again later, or update DEX_VERSION near the top of this page's script.
